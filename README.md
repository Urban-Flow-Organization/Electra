# Electra

The ELECTRA platform is a sophisticated SmartMobility solution developed to centralize and automate the management of electric bus fleet charging within a unified framework. Designed to act as a high-performance depot manager, the system ensures that vehicles are consistently prepared for service through the integration of advanced optimization algorithms and real-time technical monitoring. And by leveraging standardized protocols and an autonomous architectural design, **ELECTRA** provides a robust foundation for efficient operations.


## Charging Management and Monitoring

This module operates as the depot's technical command center, integrating advanced telemetry to supervise each individual charging process. Its technical capacity allows for a minute-by-minute analysis of voltage and current values. In addition to visualizing the State of Charge (SoC), the system monitors critical real-time variables such as voltage, amperage, and component temperature. To streamline operations, smart visual indicators provide immediate warnings regarding preconditioning failures or anomalous power consumption automatically detected by the system.

## Intelligent Planning
The platform acts as an autonomous energy manager designed to ensure the fleet is service-ready with maximum efficiency. The core of the system is a multi-criteria optimization algorithm that balances three priorities: minimizing departure delays, reducing energy costs by leveraging tariff periods, and avoiding power peaks that exceed contracted limits. ELECTRA calculates the remaining available power in the depot in real-time, integrating data from transformers and network analyzers via a SCADA process. Once the plan is established, the system automatically sends dynamic charging profiles to each connector, adjusting the supply to the physical constraints of both the charger and the vehicle without the need for manual intervention.

## Control Center and Incidents

This module serves as a proactive surveillance system that allows for the anticipation of logistical problems before they impact vehicle departures. Its most prominent feature is the calculated alarms engine, which projects the future charging status and warns if a bus will not reach its battery target in time for its next service. The interface centralizes infrastructure health monitoring, overseeing everything from VPN communication availability to the status of thermal imaging cameras in fire zones. Consequently, operators are provided with a structured view of incidents, classified by criticality levels and linked to each unit's departure time, enabling the efficient prioritization of corrective actions
